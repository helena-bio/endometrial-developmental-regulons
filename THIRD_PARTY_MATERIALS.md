# Third-party and upstream materials

This repository records provenance for data, identifiers, annotations,
regulatory relationships, subtype labels, gene sets, and other materials
obtained from external sources.

The presence of an external record in this repository does not transfer
copyright, database rights, data-use conditions, or other rights to
Helena Bioinformatics, the authors, or repository users.

## General rule

Third-party and upstream-derived materials remain subject to the terms of
their original providers.

Repository users are responsible for:

- obtaining any required source-data access;
- reviewing the current source terms and policies;
- complying with citation and attribution requirements;
- respecting controlled-access and participant-data restrictions;
- determining whether their planned reuse is permitted.

The repository licences apply only to material for which the licensors hold
the necessary rights.

## Source categories

External source categories represented in this study include:

- TCGA and GDC records and identifiers;
- CPTAC and PDC records and identifiers;
- cBioPortal public metadata;
- published molecular-subtype annotations;
- CollecTRI regulatory relationships;
- reference gene annotations and identifiers;
- published supplementary materials and source workbooks;
- external gene sets and biological annotations.

This list identifies broad source categories. It does not replace the
detailed provenance and acquisition records stored with each analysis
package.

## Derived materials

Some repository tables are original analytical outputs derived from
upstream material.

CC-BY-4.0 applies only to the original selection, organisation, analysis,
expression, or presentation over which the licensors hold rights. It does
not replace rights or conditions attached to the underlying source.

## Restricted data

Controlled-access and restricted source data are not distributed through
this repository.

Public identifiers, manifests, checksums, and provenance records do not
themselves grant access to restricted data.

## No endorsement

Use of source names, identifiers, or citations does not imply that an
upstream provider endorses this study, repository, interpretation, or
downstream reuse.

## Where to inspect provenance

Before reusing repository material, consult:

- `docs/data-provenance.md`;
- `data/README.md`;
- protocol and provenance files in each analysis package;
- source-specific acquisition and linkage records;
- `release/file-manifest.tsv` and `release/SHA256SUMS`.
